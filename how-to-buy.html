How to Buy

The Celestial Firmament is sold direct and through a worldwide network of accredited partners. Whether you are licensing a single workstation for a planetarium kiosk or rolling out a fleet for a permanent installation, the path to purchase is the same in four short steps.

Four steps to your license

Step 01

Find a partner

Locate an accredited reseller in your region using the partner directory below, or contact us directly for territories without a local partner.

Step 02

Request a quote

Share your project scope — number of seats, deployment surface, support tier — and your partner will return a tailored quotation within two business days.

Step 03

Place your order

Confirm the quote, sign the licence agreement, and complete payment through your partner. Educational and non-profit pricing is available on request.

Step 04

Activate & deploy

You will receive licence keys and download links by email within one business day. Activation is one-click and works fully offline after first use.

Frequently asked

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. The Observer edition is free for personal use and shares the same render engine as the commercial editions. For commercial trials, contact your partner for a 30-day evaluation key.

Is the licence perpetual or subscription?

Composer is sold as an annual subscription that includes all updates and email support. Firmament is offered as a perpetual site licence with an optional annual maintenance plan.

Do you offer education or non-profit pricing?

Accredited educational institutions, museums and registered non-profits qualify for a 40% discount on Composer and bespoke pricing on Firmament. Ask your partner or write to sales directly.

What about offline activation?

All editions can activate offline using a one-time challenge file. Firmament additionally supports an on-premise activation server for air-gapped deployments.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10/11, macOS 13 and later, and Ubuntu 22.04 LTS. WebGL 2.0 capable hardware is required; a discrete GPU is recommended for 4K and dome output.

No partner in your territory, or a project that needs special attention? Our sales team is happy to help and will route you to the closest partner or handle the order in-house.
