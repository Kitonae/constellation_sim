# Constellation Sim - App Summary & Suno Music Prompt

## App Summary

**Constellation Map: The Celestial Firmament** is an interactive 3D web planetarium that lets users explore the night sky. Built with Three.js and WebGL, it renders all 88 classical constellations, 9 planets with textured surfaces, thousands of twinkling stars, a Milky Way particle band, fractal nebulae, and animated meteor showers.

The experience is deeply meditative and contemplative. Users float alone through space, orbiting a celestial sphere with smooth camera controls. As they gaze toward a constellation, it brightens and reveals its name, mythological translation (e.g., Orion - "The Hunter"), and a poetic description blending Greco-Roman mythology with astronomy. A demo mode offers an automated tour through all constellations with cinematic camera transitions.

The visual aesthetic is dark and luminous: deep midnight-blue void, soft blue-white glowing text, bloom effects on stars, a vignette darkening the edges of view, and fractal nebulae in ethereal purples, blues, and oranges. Planet textures are rendered in realistic detail. Everything is designed to evoke awe, wonder, and serene isolation in the vastness of the cosmos.

The app includes a procedurally generated ambient soundtrack built entirely from the Web Audio API -- detuned sawtooth drones, sub-bass pulses, evolving minor chord pads, pentatonic shimmer arpeggios, and band-passed noise sweeps processed through reverb. The mood is cosmic, timeless, and hypnotic.
